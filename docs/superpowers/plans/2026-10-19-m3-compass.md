> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace milestone 2's floating handheld with **the compass** on `/lab/compass/` (NL) and `/en/lab/compass/` (EN), a Chinese luopan in a dark purple void.
- **The loader:** a flat SVG dial whose needle hunts for north while the site loads, then hands over unseen to the live 3D compass.
- **The pearl:** the visitor's cursor is a glowing pearl that the needle chases. A drag spins the dial freely; it leans toward the pearl.
- **The scroll:** it pushes into the heaven pool, where the real portrait shows, then swings the needle through Bob's three homes, each painting itself around the outer ring: China, then Aruba, then Rotterdam's Erasmus bridge.

Bob reviews this before milestone 4 turns the compass into a hard drive.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure, tested modules decide everything that moves:**
  - `Choreography` maps scroll progress to a pose.
  - `CameraRig` fits the dial to the layout.
  - `AngleSpring` moves the needle, `FreeTurn` the drag spin, and `PearlTarget` the pearl.
  - `loading` drives the loader.
- **Three-free modules** (`dialSpec`, `glyphs`, `dialSvg`, `loading`, `AngleSpring`) feed both the build-time SVG loader and the 3D scene, so the two always match.
- **`instrument/*`** builds the compass parts with parametric rings and canvas-painted faces.
- **`CompassScene`** owns the renderer, light, pearl, quality ladder and warm-up.
- **`CompassStageController`** wires scroll, Lenis, the pointer, focus and drag, using milestone 2's controller pattern.
- **A tiny entry module** runs the loader and imports three.js as a separate chunk.
- **Milestone 2's hub is removed** once the compass page replaces it.

  - `d` = the circular distance between `u` and `origin`, in [0, 0.5]; `front = sweep·(0.5 + soft)`.

```ts
  pointerAt(x: number, y: number): void; touchAt(x: number, y: number): void; focusAt(x: number, y: number): void; wake(): void; // the pearl

  debugState(): { section: string; local: number; step: number; spin: number; bearing: number; pearl: [number, number]; rim: { x: number; y: number; diameter: number } };
  dispose(): void;
}
```

**Values:**
- **Quality ladder:**
  - Fine pointer: `{1.75,true,400}` → `{1.75,true,200}` → `{1.75,false,200}` → `{1.25,false,150}` → `{1,false,100}`.
  - Coarse pointer: `{1.25,true,150}` → `{1.25,false,100}` → `{1,false,80}`.
  - The budget uses `PixelBudget({ limit: budgetLimit(idle, 1/56), slowRun: 3 })`, as the hub did.
  - The warm-up also compiles a clearcoat-off stand-in, so stepping down never stalls.
- **Renderer:** antialias, clear `#07040d`, NeutralToneMapping at exposure 1, no shadows.
- **Light:**
  - A RoomEnvironment through PMREM, with its walls darkened to `0x100b16` but its light panels kept, `environmentIntensity` 0.8.
  - A key DirectionalLight `0xffe2b8` at 2.2 from (−3, 4, 5).
  - A rim light `0x8a5cff` at 1.6 from (2, −1, −4).
  - These are starting values: Task 6 tunes them against `refs\compass-hero.png` and records the final numbers in the code.
- **Space:**
  - Backdrop direction (0, −0.15, −1), behind the dial.
  - Haze as it is.
  - Dust in the box (−4, −3, −4)…(4, 3, 2).
  - FogExp2 `#160c26` at 0.03.
- **Pearl:**
  - A sprite (glow texture, size 0.07) plus an emissive core (r 0.022) plus a PointLight `0xffd27a` at 2.5 (distance 3, decay 2). It is always in the scene, so it never recompiles.
  - It sits on a camera-facing plane through the point 0.6·rim in front of the face centre.
  - Its screen position damps toward `PearlTarget.at()` with λ 10.
  - **Modes:**
    - Fine pointer: rest until the first pointer move.
    - Coarse: drift until touched; a touch holds 1.5 s, then the drift resumes.
    - Focus holds until the next input.
    - Idle: 20 s after the last input, the drift pauses.
- **Needle:**
  - target = `blendBearing(pointerBearing ?? last, scripted (rad), pose.needle.weight)`.
  - `AngleSpring.step(target, dt)`.
- **Turn, lean and tip:**
  - `FreeTurn.step(dt, tipLimit(framing.tilt))` gives spin (on `spin.rotation.y`) and tip.
  - **The lean** toward the pearl, at most 5°: `root.rotation.y = lean·ndcX`, `root.rotation.x = −lean·ndcY + tip`.
- **Camera:**
  - Normally `placeCamera` with `{ layout: dialLayout(w,h), ...pose.framing }`.
  - During the intro, `lerpShot(matched, current, smootherstep(t/1.2))`.
  - After `matchDial`, until `startIntro`, the matched shot.
- **Progress:** eased by damp 8, as in the hub; `immediate` sets it at once. The ring pattern and the needle are set from the eased pose every frame.
- **Sleep:** when `PearlTarget.idle` and the spring, turn and progress are settled, nothing is drawn and the dust and haze time stops, until `wake()` or new input.
- **Loading:**
  - `onLoad` reports textures (0→1 as files settle) and warm (0→1 across painting and `compileAsync`).
  - `ready()` resolves after both reach 1, with no frame before.
- **`paintInstrument`:**
  - Painted at `STRIP.fine` or `STRIP.coarse`, with the face at 2048² (fine) or 1024² (coarse).
  - `colorSpace` sRGB on colour canvases; linear on `mr`.
  - Anisotropy 8.
