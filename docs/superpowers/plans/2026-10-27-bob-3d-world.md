# The 3D World Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Bob's standing rule for this project: no subagents, so this plan runs inline (executing-plans).

**Goal:** The projects' space becomes a 3D nebula the light and camera fly through (the fall included), the light flies straight to rest beside each bead with no circling, a click on a bead takes the visitor inside it with two clear ways on, and the beads float, turn for the pointer and wear real gold.

**Architecture:** The path loses its ORBIT and INSIDE units: each project is a TRAVEL and an ARRIVE (`src/state/chapters.ts`, `src/scene/bob/lightPath.ts`). The inside becomes a mode, not a place on the scroll: a click flies the focus camera (`src/scene/bob/focus.ts`, the close view that the project page already stands over) into the bead's inside, kept in the router's history so Back closes it. The nebula is a new pure rules module and a three.js builder beside the engraved way's, posed by the Void each frame; the beads' gold is modelled in Blender in the beads' file.

**Tech Stack:** React Router 8 (prerendered), R3F 9.8, three 0.186, zustand, Vitest, tsx; Blender 5.2 headless for the beads' file; the proof harness in Playwright-core headless Edge.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-bob-nebula-space-design.md` (revision 2, 9d0b0f3)

## Global Constraints

- Dark, calm, one focal point: the light and the bead it is at.
- Luma gates: every still on the way (fall, trips, arrivals, look back) mean 0.11 at most; the bead's inside 0.15 at most; aim at the first mock-up's 0.04 to 0.07.
- Behind each fact's words on a phone the picture stays dark (the harness's 0.05 check stays).
- The canvas sleeps when nothing has moved for a while: the float's clock stops `IDLE_SECONDS` (12 s) after the last input.
- No new lights; the beads' file under about 1.6 MB; no large downloads (clouds from shaders, canvas textures and points).
- Reduced motion: no float, no turn; the inside opens and closes by the step fade.
- Way 2: up to five projects on the way, each a TRAVEL and an ARRIVE.
- NL first, EN at /en/; never invent facts about Bob; the Cookies Clicker classmate is never named.
- No subagents; nothing merged or pushed without Bob; never edit `.impeccable/config.json`; never use port 4399.
- Commit trailer: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **Getting out of a bead on a phone:** the visitor inside a bead on a 390 × 844 touch screen leaves with one tap of "Terug", with the browser's Back, and with a swipe; never stuck. Pinned in Task 2 (unit) and Task 7 (harness `inside`, touch).
2. **A click on a bead the light is not at:** on the way, only the bead the light rests beside opens on a click (the one ahead is dim and not yet the visitor's); in the look back any bead opens. Pinned in Task 2 (`opensOnClick`).
3. **Input during the flight in:** a scroll or Esc while the camera is still flying in turns it back out at once, from where it is, with no jump. Pinned in Task 2.
4. **A reload or a direct arrival with the inside in the history:** the page comes back at the arrival, not stuck half inside. Pinned in Task 2 (`insideFromHistory` reads only a state the session pushed) and Task 7.
5. **Five projects:** every arrival frames its whole bead and the look back frames them all. Pinned in Task 1.
