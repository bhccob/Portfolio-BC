# The Stack, Second Round Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Bob's standing rule for this project: no subagents, so this plan runs inline (executing-plans).

**Goal:** Five changes Bob chose from mock-ups, built into the site:
- the Q&A as a gold ring round the closing stack, turned a question at a time;
- a clicked project opening as its own full-screen page over the stack;
- three gyroscope rings and realistic stars round the stack;
- the falling light as a signal on a circuit, through a richer space;
- the B letters made like the compass's bands.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure modules hold the places and the moves**, as before:
  - the chapters gain a QA unit per mark (`src/state/chapters.ts`);
  - the scroll steps through them (`src/input/target.ts`, `scrollLoop.ts`);
  - the path gains a `qa` part (`src/scene/bob/worldPath.ts`) with the ring's helpers (`src/scene/bob/qaRing.ts`);
  - the circuit's lanes come from the light's trail (`src/scene/bob/circuit.ts`).
- **The 3D is built in code** from the mock-ups' makes: the ring (`ring.ts`), the decor stars (`decorStars.ts`), the realistic stars (`stars.ts`), the gyroscope (`gyro.ts`), the light (`light.ts`, rewritten) and the B's bands (`letterBands.ts` with `engraving.ts`), all under `src/engine/bob/build/` except `letterBands.ts` (`src/engine/bob/`).
- **The page's words are DOM:**
  - the Q&A overlay (`src/ui/QaRing.tsx`) and the project page (`src/ui/ProjectView.tsx`);
  - both are written each frame by the scene's writers, as the names and captions are now.

**Tech Stack:** React Router 8 (prerendered), R3F 9.8, three 0.186, postprocessing 6.39.5, zustand, Vitest (node), tsx, sharp (already in node_modules) for the pictures; the proof harness in Playwright-core headless Edge.

## Review Focus

1. **A flick of many wheel notches, or a long swipe, in the Q&A** takes exactly one step (one question), never two. Input during a turn's lock (`QA_LOCK_MS`) is dropped, not queued. Pinned in Task 3 (`'takes one step for a burst of notches'`).
2. **A resize from laptop to phone at a question** keeps the place (the same question). It re-frames the ring's view and moves the answer below the ring. Pinned in Task 4 (`ringShot` per aspect) and Task 8 (the resize test).
3. **A project in the Q&A:**
   - nothing opens while the stack is closing or closed (`opensOnClick` is false in `qa`);
   - a project page reached directly is still opened at its board's stop.

   Pinned in Task 4.
4. **Missing content:**
   - Porfael has no pictures, so its page goes from its words straight to the next project;
   - a question Bob has not answered shows the placeholder with its tag on the ring, as on the end page.

   Pinned in Task 7 and Task 13.
5. **3D switched off at a question, at the end, or with a project page open** leaves no `data-qa`, `data-qa-at` or `data-inside` behind and closes the view. The end page then stands in the flow and is usable. Pinned in Task 8 and Task 14.
