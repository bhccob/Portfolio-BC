# BOB · the light dive: down past five facts, into the projects

*2026-10-25. Builds on the BOB compass design (`2026-10-21-bob-compass-design.md`), especially §2.3 ("The dive", "The scroll's stretches", "The pace") and §2.4, and on the end page design (`2026-10-24-bob-end-page-design.md`). Everything they say still holds unless this document says otherwise.*

- "The national titles were in Aruba."
- "i want the text to feel a part of the light thats dropping down"

Today the dive from BOB seen from above is one scroll stop: the camera drops through the O, down a short glowing tunnel and out under the glass into the void, where the three projects wait. It is quick and says nothing about Bob. He wants that way down to become a journey of its own: a visitor follows a light down, meets a few facts about him one at a time, and arrives among the projects. Once there, a visitor who wants to reach him should be able to skip straight to the questions and the message form.

## Goals

1. **The dive becomes a descent.** From BOB seen from above, scrolling down drops the camera through the O, as today. Then it goes on down a dark shaft under the glass, following a point of gold light, and opens into the void at the overview of the three projects.
   - The descent follows the scroll continuously, with no stops inside it, like the rise from the home to BOB. A visitor can stop anywhere in it, for example to read a fact, and it waits there.
   - Scrolling back up runs it in reverse, at once and from wherever it stands.
   - It ends on the overview stop. From the overview on, the scroll comes to rest on stops exactly as now: the overview, Funcial, Jontai Energy, Cookies Clicker, and the end page.
   - It is reached by every input that reaches the rise: the wheel, the trackpad, a swipe, and the keys. The arrows, Page Down and Space move down the descent as they move along the rise. End and Home keep their meaning.
2. **Five facts, one at a time, written by the light.** On the way down, Bob's five facts appear in his order, each in its own part of the descent.
   - **The text is part of the falling light** (Bob: "i want the text to feel a part of the light thats dropping down").
     - As the light reaches a fact's place, a thin arc of its current branches off into the letters.
     - The current runs through them like electricity through wire and lights them gold from within, so the light writes each fact as it passes.
     - While the fact is read, a faint thread of current still joins it to the light.
     - When the light has gone on, the current leaves the letters and they fade into the dark.
   - **Never two at once.** Each fact stays readable while the visitor scrolls past it, and fades behind before the next one is lit.
   - **They take turns on either side of the light,** so the eye keeps following the light down.
   - **They are small, real 3D text in the scene.** The type is the site's title face (Unbounded): the first part heavy and gold, the rest light, as in the site's titles. The letters have depth, are lit gold from within like the engraving, and turn and drift a little as the camera passes. They are not flat text pasted over the canvas.
   - **They are readable** at 1440×900, at 1366×657 and on a 390×844 phone. On a phone they sit where they fit the width, and the long second fact may wrap to three lines.
   - The copy, made formal from Bob's words:

     | | NL | EN |
     | --- | --- | --- |
     | 1 | 20 jaar. | 20 years old. |
     | 2 | Chinees van afkomst, geboren en getogen op Aruba, woont in Nederland. | Chinese by descent, born and raised in Aruba, living in the Netherlands. |
     | 3 | Hakka-erfgoed. | Hakka heritage. |
     | 4 | Oud-nationaal kampioen powerliften van Aruba. | Former national powerlifting champion of Aruba. |
     | 5 | Oud-nationaal kampioen tafeltennis van Aruba. | Former national table tennis champion of Aruba. |

3. **The light leads the way, as electricity.** One small electric spark moves ahead of the camera down the shaft, as in `lightdive-e1.webp`. Bob: "the light can be electricity-like too representing my mechatronic role".
   - It has a glowing gold core, fine branching arcs crackling around it, and a thin wavering trail of current behind it.
   - The arcs are delicate filaments that shift slowly. They never strobe or flash, and the rest of the shaft stays dark.
   - At the bottom it comes out into the void with the visitor, and the needle takes up its pointer role on Funcial, as it does today.
4. **The skip button: "Snel naar vragen ↓" / "Skip to questions ↓".**
   - **Where it shows.** It shows while the story is among the projects: from the overview stop through Cookies Clicker's stop, the moves between them included. It is gone everywhere else: in the rise, the descent, the move into the night, at the end page, and on the project pages.
   - **What it does.** One click, tap, or Enter takes the visitor straight to the end page by the step fade (out, the story placed at the end page, in), never by travelling through the beads. On arrival the focus moves to the end page's title, as the failed void's "Vragen & contact" link already does.
   - **How it looks and behaves.** It sits at the bottom right in the site's label style: a small pill with a gold hairline, as in the mock-up. It sits above the phone's safe area and never covers a bead's label. It is a real button: the keyboard reaches it, it shows the site's focus ring, and a screen reader reads it.
5. **The void's loading hides behind the descent.**
   - **The hold moves.** Today the dive holds in the tunnel until the void's files are ready. Now the facts play regardless, and only the step out into the void waits: the light holds at the shaft's bottom until the void is ready, then goes on. A visitor who scrolls slowly never meets the hold.
   - **The descent loads separately.** The shaft, the light and the facts must not wait on the void's file.
6. **Both languages.** NL at `/` and EN at `/en/`, with every word in the dictionaries.
7. **The facts are in the HTML.** They are server-rendered in the home's page as a short list, so screen readers and crawlers have them and they exist without JavaScript.
   - With 3D on, the list is visually hidden, the way `.home-nav` is, because the 3D text cannot be read by a screen reader.
   - With 3D off, no WebGL, or the fallback card, the list shows in the home's normal flow before the project links.

## Non-goals

- The project pages' 3D interiors (M3), which follow this.
- Any change to the loader, the home, the rise, BOB seen from above, the beads, opening a project, or the end page, apart from:
  - the skip button arriving at the end page;
  - every stop after the descent moving further down the scroll, which shifts their place but not what they do.
- Lite, contact and sound.
- Facts beyond Bob's five, and random selection: with five facts, every visitor sees all five, in order.

## Anti-goals

These would make it a failure even if it worked:
- **A changed or invented fact.** The five are Bob's own, made formal and nothing more.
- **A busy or bright moment:**
  - two facts on screen at once;
  - big type;
  - anything flashing, including electricity that strobes, flickers hard or throws bright sparks across the screen;
  - the light outshining the text;
  - motion behind a fact while it is being read, beyond the slow drift of the descent itself.
- **A chore or a trap:**
  - a descent so long that reaching the projects feels like work;
  - a scroll that fights the visitor or will not go back up;
  - a fact that cannot be read on a phone.
- **A stall with nothing to look at** while the void loads.
- **The skip button anywhere but among the projects,** or covering a bead or its label.

## My calls (Bob may change any)

- **A live preview before the build** (Bob's standing rule: visuals before any build). The plan opens with a throwaway, real-time preview of the descent for his OK, and the full build waits for it. The preview covers:
  - the camera's path through the O and down the shaft;
  - the spark and its arcs;
  - one fact written by the current, at 1440×900 and on a phone.
- **The spark is a light of its own.** Seen from straight above, today's needle reads as a gold disc, not as the point and thread of the frames. So the spark leads the way down by itself. At the bottom it runs into the needle's tip as the needle comes into the void, the tip lights, and the needle swings to Funcial.
- **The letters load with the home's scene.**
  - The facts' letters are made ahead of time from the site's title face, only the characters the five facts use, in both languages.
  - They ship with the scene the loader already waits for, so they are there the moment the home lands.
  - The descent adds at most about 150 KB compressed to that load. If the letters cannot fit in that, the builder raises it rather than adding a second hold.
  - If the 3D letters ever fail, the descent still runs and the facts stay in the HTML list (goal 7).
- **The descent's pace.**
  - It follows the scroll with a gentle lag, about 0.45 s, between the rise's 0.25 s and the old dive's 1.1 s. So it comes to rest within about a second and a half after the hand stops, and a fact stays where the visitor stopped to read it.
  - How fast the visitor gets to the projects is theirs: a slow scroll reads every fact, and a quick flick may pass them.
  - A key moves about one fact's length.
  - End, Home and the skip button keep their own moves, as now.
  - The number is tuned in the preview.
- **How the light writes a fact.** A branch of current runs into the letters, a thread stays while the fact is read, and the current leaves as the light goes on (goal 2).
- **About four screen heights of scroll** from BOB seen from above to the overview: one through the O, about half a screen per fact, and a short one out into the void. The builder may tune it within the pace, as long as each fact can be read at an ordinary scroll speed.
- **Bob's order, every visit.** Bob first said "random". With his five facts and "one by one", every visitor sees all five, in his order.
- **The wording in the table.** "Chinees van afkomst" for "Chinese by blood". "Hakka-erfgoed" / "Hakka heritage" for "heritage hakka". "Van Aruba" on both titles.
- **"20 jaar" is fixed text.** It needs changing on Bob's birthday. Counting up by itself would need his birth month and year, which he has not given.
- **The button.** Its words and the ↓; a fade rather than a flight to the end page; the focus moving to the end page's title.
- **Reduced motion.** The descent becomes steps: BOB seen from above, then each fact, then the overview, each by the step fade, so the facts are still read.
- **The facts as an HTML list** for screen readers and 3D off (goal 7).
- **A failed void.**
  - The descent and its facts still play, and the story holds at the shaft's bottom where the void would open.
  - The home nav, with its "Vragen & contact" link, shows there as it shows at the hold today.
  - The skip button does not show, because there are no projects to be among.
