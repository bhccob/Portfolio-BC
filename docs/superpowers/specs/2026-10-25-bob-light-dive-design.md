## Goals

1. **The dive becomes a descent.**
   - **Where it runs.** From BOB seen from above, scrolling down drops the camera through the O, as today. Then it goes on down a dark shaft under the glass behind a falling light, and comes out into the void at the overview of the three projects.
   - **It follows the scroll continuously,** with no stops inside it, like the rise from the home to BOB. A visitor can stop anywhere and it waits there.
   - **Scrolling back up runs it in reverse,** at once and from wherever it stands.
   - **It ends on the overview stop.** From the overview on, the scroll comes to rest on stops exactly as now: the overview, Funcial, Jontai Energy, Cookies Clicker, and the end page.
   - **Every input that reaches the rise reaches it:** the wheel, the trackpad, a swipe, and the keys. The arrows, Page Down and Space move down the descent as they move along the rise. End and Home keep their meaning.
2. **The light falls, and the camera stays close on it.**
   - It comes in from above and falls down the screen, its long, smooth, softly wavering trail streaming up above it, as in `lightdive-d1.webp`.
   - It is an orb of gold light with a soft white-gold core and a gentle halo. It is fairly large in the picture: the camera is close and focused on it.
   - A few delicate electric filaments flicker around it, for Bob's mechatronics, without ever strobing or flashing.
   - Around it, thin streaks of soft violet and faint gold fall with it, like a slow, quiet rain of light, sparse, with a lot of dark between them.
   - Only gold and violet are used, on the site's near-black. It is calmer than the reference clip: the light flows, the streaks drift softly, and the shaft stays dark.
3. **Bob's five facts ride beside the light, one after another.**
   - **One at a time, without a gap.** Each fact is shown while the light falls through its part of the descent, travelling with the light, held to it by a fine gold line from the light's side. Then it dims and lets go as the next one comes up on the line, so the stream runs continuously from the first fact to the void.
   - **Small, thin, quiet labels,** in the site's label face (Martian Mono, a light weight, capitals, widely tracked), with a small number above each (01 to 05), as in `bob-light-dive-6.html`. They are never big, heavy or fiery.
   - **The glow is synced to the light.** The words glow softly in the light's colour and follow its brightness: when the light swells or its filaments flicker, the words do too.
   - **They are readable** at 1440×900, at 1366×657 and on a 390×844 phone. The long second fact runs over three short lines.
   - The copy, made formal from Bob's words. It is shown as capitals, with a line break where the label needs one.

     | | NL | EN |
     | --- | --- | --- |
     | 01 | 20 jaar | 20 years old |
     | 02 | Chinees van afkomst / geboren en getogen op Aruba / woont in Nederland | Chinese by descent / born and raised in Aruba / living in the Netherlands |
     | 03 | Hakka-erfgoed | Hakka heritage |
     | 04 | Oud-nationaal kampioen powerliften van Aruba | Former national powerlifting champion of Aruba |
     | 05 | Oud-nationaal kampioen tafeltennis van Aruba | Former national table tennis champion of Aruba |

4. **The arrival: the light becomes a free-floating needle (Bob's pick, A).**
   - **No burst.** At the bottom, the light flows out of the shaft into the violet void and becomes a slender gold needle that floats free in the space, with a small point of light at its tip.
   - **It points at Funcial from a distance.** Nothing joins the needle to a bead: no line, no beam, no ribbon. The bead it points at answers with a soft glow along its gold halo band and porthole, and the others stay dim.
   - **Scrolling on turns the needle to the next bead,** as the needle does now. The bead stops, their labels, opening a project and the way to the end page work as now.
   - **It replaces today's large pointer in the void.** Today the needle comes down large at the foot of the picture. Now it is this smaller, free-floating one.
5. **The skip button: "Snel naar vragen ↓" / "Skip to questions ↓".**
   - **Where it shows.** It shows while the story is among the projects: from the overview stop through Cookies Clicker's stop, the moves between them included. It is gone everywhere else: in the rise, the descent, the move into the night, at the end page, and on the project pages.
   - **What it does.** One click, tap, or Enter takes the visitor straight to the end page by the step fade (out, the story placed at the end page, in), never by travelling through the beads. On arrival the focus moves to the end page's title, as the failed void's "Vragen & contact" link already does.
   - **How it looks and behaves.** It sits at the bottom right in the site's label style: a small pill with a gold hairline. It sits above the phone's safe area and never covers a bead's label. It is a real button: the keyboard reaches it, it shows the site's focus ring, and a screen reader reads it.
6. **The void's loading hides behind the descent.**
   - **The hold moves.** Today the dive holds in the tunnel until the void's files are ready. Now the light and the facts play regardless, and only the step out into the void waits: the light holds at the shaft's bottom until the void is ready, then flows on. A visitor who scrolls at an ordinary pace never meets the hold.
   - **The descent loads separately.** The shaft, the light and the labels must not wait on the void's file.
7. **Both languages.** NL at `/` and EN at `/en/`, with every word in the dictionaries.
8. **The facts are in the HTML.** They are server-rendered in the home's page as a short list, so screen readers and crawlers have them and they exist without JavaScript.
   - With 3D on, the list is visually hidden, the way `.home-nav` is. Moving text is no way for a screen reader to read facts.
   - With 3D off, no WebGL, or the fallback card, the list shows in the home's normal flow before the project links.

## Non-goals

- The project pages' 3D interiors (M3), which follow this.
- Any change to the loader, the home, the rise, BOB seen from above, the beads, opening a project, or the end page. The exceptions are:
  - the needle's new free-floating look among the beads (goal 4);
  - the skip button arriving at the end page;
  - every stop after the descent moving further down the scroll, which shifts their place but not what they do.
- Lite, contact and sound.
- Facts beyond Bob's five, and random selection: every visitor sees all five, in his order.

## Anti-goals

These would make it a failure even if it worked:
- **A changed or invented fact.** The five are Bob's own, made formal and nothing more.
- **Childish text:** big, heavy, fiery or cartoon letters. The facts are quiet labels (Bob: "the text looks too childish").
- **A busy or bright moment:**
  - two facts at once;
  - a strobe, a hard flicker or a flash, the clip's burst included;
  - colour beyond gold and violet;
  - the light outshining the words or the shaft losing its dark.
- **Anything physically joining the needle or the light to a bead** (Bob: "i dont like that design that the thing is physically connected to the beads").
- **A chore or a trap:**
  - a descent so long that reaching the projects feels like work;
  - a scroll that fights the visitor or will not go back up;
  - a fact that cannot be read on a phone.
- **A stall with nothing to look at** while the void loads.
- **The skip button anywhere but among the projects,** or covering a bead or its label.

## Left to the builder

- **How the labels are drawn.** For example, as DOM text placed each frame at the light's projected position, or as text in the scene, within the CSP (no workers) and the canvas's frame budget. Their glow must follow the light.
- **The descent's place in the code.** It goes in the chapter and stop structure as a continuous stretch like the rise, between BOB seen from above and the overview; the builder decides that and how the stops after it move.
- **The scene.** The camera's path through the O and down the shaft; the shaft's look; how the light, its filaments, its trail and the streaks are drawn (geometry, shaders or sprites); and where each label sits on each screen size.
- **The free needle.** Its model and size, how the light turns into it, and how it turns between beads.
- **The hold and the failed void.** How the hold moves to the shaft's bottom, and how the failed void reaches the same place.
- **The exact lengths and timings,** within the pace.
- **The harness steps that prove it,** by real input at the three sizes:
  - the descent and its reverse;
  - the five facts one at a time and in order, never two;
  - stopping mid-way;
  - the keys;
  - the hold;
  - the failed void;
  - reduced motion;
  - 3D off;
  - EN;
  - the needle never touching a bead;
  - the skip button's place, its fade and its focus.
