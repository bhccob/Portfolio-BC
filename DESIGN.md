# Design System: bhccob.me

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Departures Board"**

The whole site is one station departures board for a single route, Aruba to Rotterdam and onward. Projects are departures, the next build is boarding, earlier work is held at the gate, and possible future roles are scheduled. Characters live on split-flap tiles that clatter into place; the board housing is a deep aubergine, the characters signal yellow, and the running prose a warm white. Behind everything sits a low-key drafting layer: a blueprint grid that bends toward the pointer, dimension lines with measurements, and thin line drawings of maths, tech and sports objects.

Density is that of a timetable: rows, rules and tiles, read left to right in columns, with generous vertical breathing room between platforms. Content is never boxed. Separation comes from 1px hairline rules, a darker band behind alternate sections, and column alignment. Data (times, codes, years, stacks) is set in a monospace; everything a passenger would read from across the hall is set in heavy extra-condensed capitals.

Motion is mechanical and short: tiles flip in 62ms half-turns with a slight stagger, so rows settle like a real board rather than fading in. Everything else eases out on one curve.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Split-flap tiles as the signature element, for the name, project numbers, departure rows, clocks, the mobile menu and the logo.
- Aubergine housing in four steps, signal yellow as the single accent, lilac and dim violet for secondary text.
- Three typefaces with strict jobs: extra-condensed display, humanist sans prose, mono data.
- Rules and hairlines instead of boxes; one 4px radius everywhere a corner shows.
- Blueprint drafting marks (grid, dimension lines, corner brackets, measurement notes) as the ambient layer.

## Colors

A dark, warm-violet board lit by one signal colour.

### Primary
- **Signal Yellow** (`signal-yellow`): the characters on the board. Flap text, primary buttons, the active language tile, emphasised words inside headings, status marks, focus rings, selection, and the drawing's dimension lines (at 40 to 75% alpha). It is the only accent.
- **Soft Signal** (`signal-yellow-soft`): the upper half of a yellow tile (logo tile, cursor head), giving the lit tile its fold.
- **Ink on Yellow** (`on-yellow`): text on any yellow surface; identical to the board colour.

### Neutral
- **Board Housing** (`board`): the page background and the default surface.
- **Deep Housing** (`board-deep`): the darker band behind alternating platforms, the mobile menu, and the terminal dialog.
- **Housing Steps** (`board-2`, `board-3`): raised housing; `board-3` is the placeholder behind images and the scrollbar thumb.
- **Tile Faces** (`tile-hi` to `tile` gradient on the top half, `tile-2` on the bottom half): the flap tile body.
- **The Split** (`split`): the near-black hairline between a tile's halves.
- **Warm Paper** (`paper`): headings, lead text, running prose and the hover face of the primary button.
- **Lilac** (`lilac`): body copy, secondary flap rows (project lines), nav links.
- **Dim Violet** (`dim`): mono labels, column heads, placeholders, held/inactive tiles.
- **Rules** (`line`, `line-strong`, `grid`): lilac at 13%, 28% and 7% alpha for dividers, field underlines and the blueprint grid.

### Semantic
- **Error Coral** (`error`): field error text and the form status line. Used only for validation.

### Terminal (scoped)
- **Terminal OK** (`terminal-ok`) and **Terminal Error** (`terminal-err`): command output inside the terminal dialog only, where the log itself prints in signal yellow. They are a code-context palette, not page accents, and never appear outside the terminal.

### Named Rules
**The One Signal Rule.** Yellow is the only accent on the board (the scoped terminal palette excepted). It marks what is live (a character, a status, a call to action, a focused field); everything else is aubergine, paper or lilac.

**The Housing Rule.** Surfaces step only within the aubergine family (`board-deep` to `board-3`). Depth comes from changing the housing tone, not from a new hue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sofia Sans Extra Condensed Variable (with Arial Narrow, Roboto Condensed)
**Body Font:** Sofia Sans Variable (with Segoe UI, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono Variable (with ui-monospace, Cascadia Mono, Consolas)

**Character:** Heavy extra-condensed capitals read like board lettering at any distance; the humanist sans keeps prose friendly and legible; the wide mono carries the timetable's codes, times and labels.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(54px, 7.6vw, 96px), 0.86, uppercase, balanced): home section titles. One or two words per title switch to signal yellow via emphasis.
- **Display variants** (same face, weight and leading; size by surface): contact page title (`display-contact`), case-study title (`display-case`), project platform titles (`display-project`), the secondary home sections Career and Engine Room (`display-secondary`), and the 404 title (`display-small`). Pick the variant for the surface; do not invent a new clamp.
- **Headline** (800, clamp(34px, 3.6vw, 52px), 0.95, uppercase): subsection heads such as the next build and route stops.
- **Title / Lead** (400 sans, clamp(20px, 1.7vw, 25px), 1.45, max 34em): the opening paragraph under a section title, in paper.
- **Quote** (400 sans, clamp(19px, 1.6vw, 23px)): the pulled thought in About, in lilac.
- **Lead small** (400 sans, clamp(17px, 1.35vw, 21px), 1.5, max 36em): Bob's own sentence at the foot of the hero.
- **Body** (400 sans, 18px, 1.6, max 38em): running text, usually in lilac.
- **Supporting text** (400 sans; 16px, 15px, 14px): the step below body for secondary prose. 16px for fact values, service and engine-room descriptions, hobby text and the held-work note; 15px for hints, handles, footer text and small headings such as the form title; 14px for form privacy, field errors and footer hints. Readable prose never goes below 14px.
- **Label** (450 mono, 12px, 0.12em tracking, uppercase; 11px for column heads, fact labels and field labels): codes, times and labels. Dim violet by default, yellow when it states a live value.
- **Code** (500 mono, 13px, 1.7): the terminal log and prompt only.
- **Button** (800 display, 21px, 0.04em tracking, uppercase; 19px in the header): every button face.
- **Annotation** (450 mono, 10px, 0.12em): drafting notes on dimension lines and line objects only, always inside `aria-hidden` decoration. It is not a text size: anything a reader needs to read or operate uses label (11px) or larger. This step is deliberately left out of the frontmatter ramp so that a 10px value on readable text keeps being flagged.

### Split-Flap Tile Scale
Tiles size by context: the font size sets the em, and each context sets tile proportions in em through width and height ratios (`--tw` about 0.72 to 0.9, `--th` about 1.08 to 1.5) plus gap and corner radius. The shipped scale, largest to smallest:
- **Final call** (`flap-final`, clamp(80px, 17vw, 260px)): the HELLO board.
- **Hero name** (`flap-hero`, clamp(64px, 12.6vw, 196px); 21vw on mobile): BOB CHUNG.
- **Lost** (`flap-lost`, clamp(56px, 12vw, 180px)): the 404 board.
- **Case number** (`flap-case`, clamp(64px, 9vw, 140px)) and **project number** (`flap-number`, clamp(64px, 8vw, 120px)): #01, #02, #03.
- **Sent** (`flap-sent`, clamp(64px, 8vw, 110px)) and **loader name** (`flap-loader`, clamp(42px, 9vw, 120px)).
- **Route code** (`flap-route`, 44px; `flap-route-compact`, 30px at 560px and below): CN, AUA, RTM.
- **Next-row title** (the `display-small` size, clamp(40px, 5vw, 72px)): the next case study at the foot of a case page.
- **Timetable** (`flap-timetable`, clamp(20px, 1.9vw, 27px)): skills on the toolkit board.
- **Role row** (`flap-role`, clamp(15px, 1.75vw, 26px)), **code tile** (`flap-code`, 26px: contact codes, loader percent), **clock** (`flap-clock`, 22px; 18px on mobile).
- **Departure row** (`flap-row`, clamp(15px, 1.32vw, 20px); 4.2vw on mobile), **line** (`flap-line`, 17px: project lines, boarding status), **status** (`flap-status`, 15px: service and hobby statuses).

### Named Rules
**The Three Jobs Rule.** Display face for anything on a tile or a sign, sans for sentences, mono only for data, codes, times and labels. Never set prose in mono or labels in the display face.

**The All-Caps Board Rule.** Display and tile text is always uppercase; prose never is.

**The Tile Scale Rule.** A new tile context picks the nearest step on the tile scale and tunes proportions through the tile ratios, not through a new font size.

## Layout

Content sits in a centred container of `min(100% - 2 × gutter, 1360px)`; the hero widens to 1440px. Sections pad vertically by `section-y`, and project platforms by clamp(56px, 6vw, 96px). The fixed header is 76px tall and anchors scroll offset at header height plus 16px.

The page reads as a timetable: the hero is a four-column departures grid (year, project, line, status) that drops to three columns at 1080px and to single stacked rows at 720px. Each project is a two-column platform (a sticky rail of tile number, line and facts at roughly 0.62fr, the main column at 1.38fr); alternate platforms mirror the order and sit on the deep band. Platforms collapse to one column at 960px, where the rail stops sticking. Fact lists are two-column rows (108px label, value) separated by hairlines.

Drafting objects and their notes hide below 900px, the desktop nav and header CTA hide below 960px in favour of a full-screen tile menu, and the terminal button hides below 420px.

## Elevation & Depth

The board is flat. Depth is tonal (housing steps and the deep band) and mechanical (the tile's top-to-bottom gradient and its split line). Shadows exist only on things that physically sit above the board.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Photo drop** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 70px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.75)`; case-study hero uses `0 50px 80px -50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)`): a long soft shadow under project screenshots only.
- **Floating panel** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the toast.
- **Dialog** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 120px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the terminal dialog, over a blurred backdrop.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Board Rule.** Text, rows, tiles and buttons carry no shadow. Only screenshots, toasts and dialogs lift.

## Shapes

One small radius (4px) for every rectangle that shows a corner: buttons, departure-row hover plates, screenshot frames, the portrait, the terminal. Flap tiles round at 0.12em of their own font size (tighter, 0.05em, for the hero name) and split horizontally at the middle with a 1px near-black seam. Toggle switches (the Off Duty gravity switch, 34 × 18px) are the one pill (999px), because a switch track is a physical slot. Language tiles round at 3px. Fields have no corners at all: they are underlines. Dividers are 1px gradient hairlines painted as backgrounds rather than borders. Drafting marks (dimension lines with end ticks, 22px corner brackets at 2px) frame images instead of boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid signal-yellow plates with heavy condensed capitals.
- **Shape:** gently cornered (4px), min height 52px (42px in the header).
- **Primary:** yellow face, aubergine text, 21px display caps, trailing arrow icon.
- **Hover / Focus:** a paper-coloured flap falls from the top over the face (scaleY 0 to 1, 0.4s ease-out), the button lifts 2px and the arrow shifts 4px right. Focus shows the global 2px yellow ring at 4px offset.
- **Text link:** paper text over a 2px yellow underline; on hover the underline retracts to the right and the text turns yellow, with a diagonal arrow nudging up-right.
- **Icon tool:** 40px square, lilac glyph; on hover it becomes a yellow plate with aubergine glyph.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** no box. A 1px `line-strong` underline, 20px paper text, yellow caret, mono uppercase label above.
- **Focus:** a 2px yellow underline draws in from the left over 0.5s and the label turns yellow.
- **Error:** the underline turns coral and a 14px coral message appears below. A mono character count sits at the right.

### Navigation
Mono uppercase links (12px, 0.12em) in lilac; hover and active turn paper and draw a 2px yellow underline from the left. The logo is a single yellow flap tile beside `bhccob.me` in mono. Language switch is two small flap tiles (30 × 34px), dim when inactive and yellow when current. On scroll the header gains a 94% board-colour fill and hides when scrolling down. Mobile: a full-screen deep-housing menu clipped open from the top, with numbered rows of large flap text staggered in at 70ms.

### Split-Flap Tile (signature)
Each character is its own tile: top half on a `tile-hi` to `tile` gradient, bottom half on `tile-2`, divided by the split seam. Tile proportions are set per use through width and height ratios (about 0.76 to 0.9 wide and 1.08 to 1.5 tall per em). Text tones are yellow (default), lilac, paper or dim. On change, the old top half falls and the new bottom half lands in 62ms half-flips, cycling through a few random characters with a 14 to 55ms stagger between tiles. Visitors can type and the hero board spells their words.

### Departure Row
A grid row of flap columns (year, project, line, status with a check, play or ring mark). Hover lays a 5% yellow plate over the row and grows a 3px yellow bar from its left edge. Held rows drop to 75% opacity with dim tiles.

### Blueprint Layer
A canvas grid at 56px steps in lilac at 4.5 to 8.5% alpha that bends toward the pointer within 240px; thin line-icon objects (lilac at 20%, one yellow accent at 38%) drift with scroll and carry mono measurement notes; yellow dimension lines label image sizes. The custom cursor is a small yellow tile with a delayed trail of tile fragments, its head on the real pointer.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put names, numbers, statuses and short codes on split-flap tiles; that is the board's voice.
- **Do** keep yellow for live things only: characters, statuses, calls to action, focus and emphasis inside a title.
- **Do** separate content with 1px hairlines (`line` or `line-strong`), column alignment and the deep band behind alternate sections.
- **Do** set times, years, stacks, codes and labels in Martian Mono uppercase at 11 to 12px with 0.12em tracking.
- **Do** frame images with drafting marks (dimension lines, corner brackets) and a 4px corner.
- **Do** respect reduced motion: flips and transitions collapse to instant.

### Don't:
- **Don't** box content in bordered cards; the terminal dialog's 1px hairline is the only stroked container.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue; status and emphasis stay yellow, secondary text stays lilac or dim.
- **Don't** put shadows on text, rows, tiles or buttons.
- **Don't** set prose in the condensed display face or in mono.
- **Don't** draw fields as boxes; inputs are underlines.
